Thank you for the review. We have added every precaution listed in

[[device, e.g. iPhone 15]] running iOS [[version]].

**1. Terms of use (EULA) before registering or logging in**
Every sign-in and sign-up screen shows an unticked checkbox: "I agree to
the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy, and there is no tolerance for
objectionable content or abusive users" (in Arabic, with links to both
documents). Signing in or registering — with a password, Sign in with
Apple or Google — is not possible until it is ticked. The Terms of Use
have a section on zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive
users, and explain reporting, blocking, and our 24-hour review.

**2. Filtering objectionable content**
Listings, reviews, story captions, service requests, offers and profile
names and descriptions containing objectionable words are refused when
they are posted. In chat, such words are replaced by "***".

**3. Flagging objectionable content**
Users can report a listing (flag button on the listing), a review
("إبلاغ" on each review), a story (⋮ on the story), and a user, company
or office (⋮ in a chat or on a profile), choosing a reason.

**4. Blocking abusive users**
Users can block another account from a chat, a company or office
profile, or a story. The blocked account's listings, stories, reviews
and chats disappear from the user's app immediately, and neither side
can message the other. Every block is also sent to our moderation team
as a report. Blocked accounts are listed in Settings → "المحظورون".

**5. Acting on reports within 24 hours**
Every report and block notifies our moderation team by email. From the
admin panel the team removes the reported content and suspends the
account that posted it in a single action. Reports are reviewed within
24 hours.

**Demo accounts** are the same as in our previous reply (individual,
company and office, on production).
